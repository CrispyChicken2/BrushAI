## VRAM estimate

- Renderer forward + backward at 512², 1k strokes, fp32, chunked compositing: ≈ 2–4 GB.
- Optimization baseline with LPIPS-VGG at 512²: ≈ +1.5 GB.
- Animation: CPU/GPU light (< 1 GB).

Comfortably within the 12 GB of the RTX 5070.

## Success criteria

- Renderer unit-tested: shapes, gradients (including gradcheck), determinism, parallel == sequential
  compositing.
- Optimization baseline reaches LPIPS < 0.25 on 512² demo images within 2000 strokes (to be measured
  and reported honestly if not met).
- Animation shows per-stroke partial reveal, no visible brush.
- 3 demo GIFs, ≤ 4 MB each, reproducible with one command.
