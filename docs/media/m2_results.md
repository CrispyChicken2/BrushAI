The GIF of the PT painting is rendered with the PT brush images at the final resolution (generic render, `docs/design/paint_transformer.md` section 14), so its last frame differs slightly from the pipeline canvas (`last_frame_mean_abs` in `outputs/m2/<name>/metrics.json`).
